# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-28

- Initial release.
- Adds the Privatus Analytics tracking script to the end of `<head>` on front-end pages.
- Settings for the site ID and the tracker modules (engage, auto, vitals, clicks, ask) in `config/extensions/privatus-boltanalytics.yaml`.
- People logged in to Bolt are not counted, unless you turn that off.
